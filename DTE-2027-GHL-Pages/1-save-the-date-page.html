What it is

Two days built for the people who teach dance

Dance Teacher Expo is Australia's biggest gathering of dance teachers and studio owners — two days of workshops, networking, a fashion show, a cocktail party and a vendor exhibition. 50+ sessions to master your craft, grow your studio and reconnect with your people.
